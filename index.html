About Us (Fabless Model)

URVARYS operates on a highly efficient Fabless manufacturing model. We are a US-based tooling brand (LLC) that integrates advanced engineering design with our dedicated precision CNC manufacturing center in Dongguan, China. This architecture allows us to maintain strict structural analysis and uncompromising quality control (QC) while delivering direct-from-factory pricing to North American fabrication shops and custom builders.
